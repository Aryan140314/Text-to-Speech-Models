# 🎙️ Voiceover Strategy & Production Guide

Recommended model choices for different production use cases.

---

## 🎯 Model Selection Strategy by Content Genre

### 1. YouTube & Technical Content
- **Recommended Model**: 🐟 **Fish Speech S2** or ⚡ **F5-TTS**
- **Reason**: 44.1kHz high resolution clarity and rapid speech pace.

### 2. Audiobooks & Storytelling
- **Recommended Model**: 🔊 **CosyVoice 3** or 🔍 **IndexTTS2**
- **Reason**: Long-form context stability and expressive timbre preservation.

### 3. Podcasts & Social Media
- **Recommended Model**: 🚀 **Chatterbox Turbo** or 🎙️ **OmniVoice**
- **Reason**: Natural conversational rhythm and diffusion tone guidance.

### 4. Multilingual Content & Dubbing
- **Recommended Model**: 🌐 **XTTS-v2**
- **Reason**: Coqui multilingual zero-shot engine with cross-lingual accent transfer.
