It was a cold evening in London, 1895. A small boy sat on the street corner. His name was Charlie Chaplin. He was only six years old. He was hungry. He was cold. His clothes were old and dirty. Charlie looked at people walking by. They did not look at him. Nobody stopped. Nobody helped. But Charlie's life was very hard from the beginning. His father left the family when Charlie was very young. His mother, Hannah, tried to take care of Charlie and his brother, Sidney. She was a singer. She sang in small theaters. But she did not earn much money. Sometimes she could not work at all. She became sick. Her voice stopped working. Without her voice, she could not sing. Without singing, there was no money. The family became very poor. They moved from house to house. Sometimes they had no house at all. They slept on the streets. Charlie was always hungry. He wore the same clothes every day. Other children laughed at him. He felt ashamed. He felt alone. When Charlie was seven, something terrible happened. His mother became very sick. She could not think clearly anymore. The government took her to a hospital for sick people. Charlie and Sidney had no parents now. They were sent to a workhouse. A workhouse was a place for poor children. It was not a nice place. The children worked hard every day. They cleaned floors. They washed clothes. They got very little food. The rooms were cold. The beds were hard. Charlie hated it there. But even in this dark time, something special happened. Charlie discovered something inside himself. He could make people laugh. When the other boys were sad, Charlie made funny faces. He walked in a silly way. He told jokes. The boys laughed. For a moment, they forgot their hunger. They forgot their sadness. Charlie felt something new. He felt important. He felt alive. It was a sunny morning in London, 1903. Charlie was now 14 years old. He walked into a small theater. The theater was looking for young actors. Charlie was nervous. His hands were shaking. But he wanted this job very much. He needed it. this job. The theater manager looked at Charlie. Can you act? He asked. Can you make people laugh? Charlie did not answer with words. He started to move. He walked like an old man. Then he walked like a drunk man. Then he pretended to fall.
